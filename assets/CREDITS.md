# Default tracks

`loop.flac` and `done.flac` come from the [Wii Channel OST
Collection](https://archive.org/details/wii-channel-ost-collection) on the
Internet Archive — Nintendo's Wii channel soundtrack.

**That collection is a fan upload, and its licensing status is unconfirmed.**
It is not an official Nintendo release, and ThinkLight's MIT license covers the
project's own code, not these two files.

They are bundled only as defaults you have to ask for: nothing plays until you
run `thinklight unmute`, and replacing either file with your own is a copy into
`~/.local/share/thinklight/` — see the Sound section of the README. If you would
rather not have them on disk at all, delete them there and drop in your own.
